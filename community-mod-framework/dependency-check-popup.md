Players often enable your mod without CMF. CMF provides a **dependency check popup** for the main menu that can offer to enable CMF or open the Workshop.

# How to ship it

1. Download `cmf-dependency-check.zip` from the [latest CMF GitHub release](https://github.com/Europa-Universalis-5-Modding-Co-op/community-mod-framework/releases).
2. Extract into **your** mod folder.
3. Keep the [metadata dependency](/community-mod-framework/overview-and-dependency.md) and Steam required-item link.

Details: [Dependency Check Popup wiki](https://github.com/Europa-Universalis-5-Modding-Co-op/community-mod-framework/wiki/Dependency-Check-Popup).
